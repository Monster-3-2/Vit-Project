The core problem addressed by this project is that many people struggle to effectively monitor and control their expenses and savings, often leading to financial stress or overspending. 
This challenge can prevent individuals from achieving their financial goals and maintaining discipline with their personal budgets.​

Problem Statement :

Individuals frequently face difficulties in tracking day-to-day spending and assessing whether their expenses align with their financial goals.
Lack of real-time insights and reminders often results in overspending or not saving enough.​
Without a systematic approach, users may lose clarity about their financial health and struggle to make informed decisions.
A budget management tool solves these issues by providing:
Expense and income tracking for daily insights.
The ability to set budget limits and receive alerts when spending exceeds the planned budget.​
Personalized feedback and tips to improve saving habits and financial discipline.​
This enables users to proactively manage their finances and work towards their monetary objectives—helping achieve sustainable financial health and confidence.
